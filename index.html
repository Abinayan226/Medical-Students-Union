Welcome to MSU - Faculty of Medicine

University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka

The Medical Students' Union (MSU) is the apex representative body for undergraduate medical students at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Jaffna. Since its inception, MSU has played a crucial role in safeguarding student rights, enhancing academic standards, maintaining welfare facilities, and driving community health initiatives throughout northern Sri Lanka.

We coordinate closely with university authorities, teaching hospital administration, and regional healthcare bodies to ensure our medical graduates are equipped with both exemplary clinical competencies and leadership capabilities.
