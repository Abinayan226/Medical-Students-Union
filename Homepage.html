Welcome to MSU - Faculty of Medicine

University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka

The Medical Students' Union (MSU) serves as the premier student representative body for medical undergraduates at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Jaffna. Established to safeguard student rights, foster academic and clinical standards, and enrich campus life, MSU bridges communication between students, faculty administration, and the healthcare community.

Through our diverse sub-units—including cultural forums, medical research clubs, sports committees, and social responsibility networks—we organize annual medical exhibitions, blood donation campaigns, inter-batch sports tournaments, and community outreach programs across the Northern Province.

Student Clubs & Organizations

Affiliated student bodies, forums, and sub-committees under MSU

JMFC

Jaffna Medical Faculty Club

Coordinates cultural festivals, annual musical night, literary competitions, and social gatherings for students and faculty members.

Established: 1982 Cultural & Social
JMFN

Medical Research & Academic Forum

Encourages undergraduate research, organizes journal clubs, medical debate competitions, and publishes student research bulletins.

Established: 2005 Academic & Research
MSC

Medical Sports Committee

Manages annual inter-batch athletics meets, badminton tournaments, cricket leagues, and inter-medical faculty games.

Established: 1980 Sports & Athletics
GMS

Green Medics Society

Promotes environmental sustainability, campus tree planting drives, eco-friendly healthcare practices, and waste management campaigns.

Established: 2018 Environment & CSR
MBDC

Medical Blood Donors Club

Manages emergency blood donation registries for Jaffna Teaching Hospital and holds annual mega donor drives.

Established: 1995 Health & Welfare
MMU

Medical Media & IT Unit

Oversees MSU official website, video coverage of university events, graphic design, and student portal maintenance.

Established: 2016 IT & Publications
